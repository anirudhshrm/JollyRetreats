# Invoice Download Feature - Quick Start Guide

## What's New? 🎉

After confirming a booking (either property or tour), users now see a beautiful confirmation screen with an **easy-to-click invoice download button**!

---

## Property Booking Invoice Download

### Step 1: Complete Your Reservation
Fill in your details and click **"Confirm Reservation"**

### Step 2: See the Confirmation Page
You'll see:
```
✓ Reservation Confirmed!
  
Your stay at [Property Name] is reserved from [Check-in] to [Check-out].

Booking Reference #: 12345

Guest: 2 | Dates: Jan 1 to Jan 5 | Total: $500.00
```

### Step 3: Download Your Invoice
Click the green **"📋 Download Invoice PDF"** button to instantly download your invoice!

#### What's in the Invoice PDF?
- ✅ Invoice number (INV-12345-2026)
- ✅ Booking reference and status
- ✅ Your guest information
- ✅ Property details and location
- ✅ Check-in/Check-out dates
- ✅ Itemized charges
- ✅ Total amount due
- ✅ Professional branding

#### File saves as: `invoice-12345-2026.pdf`

## Troubleshooting

### My download didn't start
- Check if popup blockers are enabled
- Try a different browser
- Refresh the page and try again
- Check your internet connection

### Where's my downloaded file?
- Check your **Downloads** folder
- Look for files starting with `invoice-`
- Check your browser's download history
- Mobile users: Check your default download location

### The invoice looks incomplete
- Zoom out in your browser (if viewing as HTML)
- Try downloading again
- Use the Preview & Print option instead

### I need to access the invoice later
- Check your email (receipt may have been sent)
- Look in your Downloads folder
- Log into your account to view booking details
